Check the exported file itself, not only the editor's preview. A cutout can look transparent while you work, then arrive with a solid background if the delivery format or export settings flatten it.

Adobe's current format help describes PNG transparency support and JPEG's matte/background handling. A filename ending in PNG does not by itself prove that transparent pixels were actually preserved.

## Choose the destination first

For an original fully clothed fashion cutout, decide whether the recipient needs to place it over another background. If so, preserve transparency in a supported format rather than exporting an opaque delivery copy by habit.

Keep a layered working document as your editable source. A delivery image and an editable master answer different needs.

## Inspect the actual export options

Use the current PNG save or export route and check the relevant transparency setting where available. Ensure that an opaque background layer is not being included unintentionally.

If you intentionally need JPEG, choose an appropriate background or matte and review that flattened result. JPEG does not preserve the same transparent cutout behavior, and renaming its extension does not convert the pixel data.

Do not treat an editor's checkerboard as part of the desired picture. A checkerboard painted into the image is visible artwork, not actual transparency.

## Reopen the file on two backgrounds

Place the exported image against both a light and a dark surface in a suitable viewer or editor. Inspect hair edges, spaces between fingers, the dress outline and the area around the shoes.

Look for an opaque rectangle, missing detail, pale fringes or dark halos. Keep the source available so you can refine the edge rather than repeatedly compressing the same flattened copy.

This guide supplies a delivery check, not a claim that an editing service always exports alpha correctly. Verify the file's actual format and pixels before handing it off. A beautiful cutout becomes more useful when it works on the background where it will really be used.
