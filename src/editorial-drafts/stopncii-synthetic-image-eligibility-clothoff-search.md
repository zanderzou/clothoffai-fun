**Synthetic imagery is not automatically outside StopNCII's scope.** Its [FAQ](https://stopncii.org/faq/) includes manipulated depictions of the submitting person, subject to its adult eligibility and access criteria. Read those criteria before assuming either that AI content is excluded or that every unwanted picture qualifies.

If a search for ClothOff brought you here, the reporting decision need not depend on proving that product created the image. This is an independent support guide, not a provider's reporting form.

## Separate two decisions

There are two questions to resolve: whether this situation fits the service and what the service can cover. A positive answer to the first is not a promise about every copy online.

| Question | What it helps establish |
| --- | --- |
| Does the situation meet the official criteria? | Whether this is an appropriate submission route |
| Is the known location a participating platform? | Whether that location is within the matching network |
| Has the platform assessed a matching post? | An outcome concerning that platform's content |

Do not use an outcome in one row as evidence for the next. A successfully created case is not a certificate that all copies have disappeared.

## Resolve uncertainty before collecting more material

Read the live eligibility questions on the official service. If the depicted age is unclear, or you do not have appropriate access to the material, seek recognized support rather than asking people to send copies. Different age-related circumstances can require a different service.

Do not submit another person's case as if you were the depicted person. You can help someone locate the official guidance without taking control of their private material or credentials. Let them decide how much assistance they want.

## Understand the platform boundary

The [service explanation](https://stopncii.org/how-it-works/) describes matching and policy review on participating platforms. It expressly limits the service's reach; it cannot remove content across the entire internet.

For an illustrative case, a harmful post on one participating service and a copy on an unrelated website are separate locations. A status change affecting the first should not be recorded as proof about the second. Keep the known host's abuse-reporting route in view.

## Make follow-up smaller

Keep private notes of the route used, case reference, relevant location and response. Save access information securely without posting it in a public help request. Avoid repeatedly searching for new copies merely to test whether the system works.

If you need help interpreting a response, state the unresolved question without forwarding the image by default. A useful handoff might ask whether the known platform participates or whether the situation needs a different reporting service. That is more precise than asking someone to promise total removal.
