Keep the original, editable document and presentation copy under different names. Adobe's Photoshop guidance describes layers and saving a separate copy. A reader-friendly name tells you which garment changed without requiring you to open every version.

## Keep the edit tied to a visible region

If you changed your blue coat to green, name the mask or adjustment “Coat color” and leave the original layer intact. Write a note that specifies which version was approved and for what use. Reopen the editable document and toggle the adjustment once to verify you can still recover the original view.

## Export the version another person will see

Give the exported copy a filename that matches the selected edit. Verify that the exported crop shows the intended full clothing and that the preview description says it was altered. Keep the source and layered file private when only the exported version is approved for sharing.
