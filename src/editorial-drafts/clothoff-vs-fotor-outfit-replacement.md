## Specify the replacement garment

Imagine your own suitable adult photo is used to preview a long-sleeved navy overshirt over an opaque cream top. The overshirt should have a straight collar, two visible pockets and closed coverage appropriate to the intended image.

Describe the garment rather than asking for an unrestricted transformation. Keep pose, face and body presentation outside the requested change. If another adult is depicted, ensure they have approved this specific edit and intended use, and follow the service's current source rules.

| Detail | Acceptance check |

| Overshirt | Fully visible navy fabric |
| Coverage | Opaque clothing remains intact |
| Collar | Matches the specified straight style |

## Inspect details that can change unnoticed

Compare the output with the original at normal size and a closer view. Count the pockets and buttons. Check whether a sleeve merges into a hand, a collar loses its shape or the cream top becomes missing or transparent.

Do not accept a result solely because the color is attractive. A plausible overall image may still contain a garment that differs from the requested item. If you revise, identify one defect and keep the rest of the brief unchanged so the next result remains assessable.

## Keep preview and product evidence separate

A generated outfit can illustrate a styling idea. It does not prove the actual fabric texture, available size or fit of a product. For retail use, retain the original product information and avoid describing invented details as real garment features.

Review the current service's image handling and deletion controls independently if using a personal photo. An appealing export is not an independent privacy assessment.
